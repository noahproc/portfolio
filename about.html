About

I'm Noah Proctor, an aspiring product manager who writes code, runs user research before writing a single spec, and cares deeply about building things people genuinely want, all while championing sustainability.

I'm currently a sophomore at Columbia University studying Operations Research with a minor in CS. I love working with numbers, asking questions, working on passion projects, and staying organized enough to manage it all. My type-A personality and love for entrenching myself in a project compels me to work in product.

One of my high school teachers described me as a Renaissance Man, an apt characterization. From literature and art to technology and mathematics, I always find myself succumbing to endless Google rabbit holes and hours worth of laptop time.

I'm most interested in working with consumer-centered products with focuses on AI, sustainability, mental health, and education services. These sectors and industries are all preferential, though I'm always open to any opportunity.
